ORDER UP! — Dapur Kilat: a fast-paced original cooking game. Customers order from six recipes (Burger Kilat, Salad Segar, Pizza Mini, Kentang Goreng, Sup Tomat, Sandwich Telur); grab nine ingredients from the counter, grill the patty, fry the potatoes, pan-fry the egg, stack the dish on the plate, and serve before patience runs out. Score with time bonuses and combos, level up every five dishes, and survive the 150-second shift — three failed orders close the kitchen. Keyboard (1–9, Space, Enter, Backspace), mouse, and touch; all art drawn on canvas, no network at runtime.
